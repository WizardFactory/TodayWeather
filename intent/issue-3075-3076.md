# Qualify seasonal weather data sources

Record reproducible paths, spatial and temporal contracts and remaining
credentials or reuse decisions for the regional products in [#3075](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/3075)
and [#3076](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/3076).
This source qualification is for server1 and the WebApp (PWA/desktop) and
native iOS and Android. Subsequent implementation can use the qualified
products with the applicable rights and coverage.
