# Hourly environmental forecasts for overseas weather

Give WebApp (PWA and desktop), native iOS and native Android access to hourly
UV, gusts, irradiance, solar energy, model severe-weather risk and WBGT through
their normal server1 weather request. Reuse the existing Visual Crossing
request. Preserve the existing forecast chart cadence and Korean behavior.
Server2 delivery is a separate phase.

Scope and acceptance are recorded in [#3071](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/3071).
