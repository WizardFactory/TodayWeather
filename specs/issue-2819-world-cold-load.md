# World cold-load outcome and aggregate bounds

Stable task: `issue-2819-world-cold-load`; frozen baseline `c1ae9082b2c86f0087727be9872b0cf82b4e3bb1`. This contract governs the implemented pre-merge candidate. Actual bounded local results are recorded in the selected verification; it is not production qualification. [Intent](../intent/issue-2819-world-cold-load.md) owns current authority; [plan](../plans/issue-2819-world-cold-load.md) owns verification and exact proposed interfaces.

## Cold-load ownership

**WC1 — one restoration outcome per admitted cohort.** The existing `CellEntry` identity owns one S07 `OwnedFlights<(), Result<(), Failure>>` with one running key, one entry and three-second completed-outcome retention. A cold caller joins it instead of creating another task waiting on `CellEntry::op`. Exactly one owner reads storage and installs Db on success. All joined callers observe that owner's result. Success is published only after Db is installed; a successful waiter re-reads the current entry Db, rather than installing a retained Db copy. Failure never installs empty or partial Db and never authorizes a VC fetch.

The flight lives on the cell entry, not a new process-wide keyed framework. Its result does not retain the entry or Db. Existing active Arc references preserve cell lock identity; existing idle LRU eviction may discard this volatile memo. Eviction/recreation is a fresh identity and may restore again. It must never revive a successful Db snapshot from an earlier evicted entry. After all owned work and callers release an entry, completed flight retention must not pin it and prevent existing idle trimming.

**WC2 — explicit owner/waiter timing.** Admission creates the owner deadline at now plus existing `WorldConfig::load` (production default 8s). It covers cell-lock wait and storage/replay work instead of restarting after lock acquisition. The owner receives this deadline; S07's outer owner timeout enforces asynchronous expiry. Each request waiter ends at its own deadline or 3s from joining, whichever comes first. A later waiter cannot extend/restart the owner deadline. The existing provider-fetch owner/backoff and gateway 3s attempt/9s public deadlines are unchanged. Tokio deadlines remain cooperative; moving synchronous CPU work or adding hard preemption is outside this slice.

Waiter timeout or cancellation does not cancel the admitted owner. The cell remains pinned through owner work. A completed storage/capacity error is retained for 3 monotonic seconds after completion, just like a successful S07 result; no new independent retry timer or retry-after map is introduced. Later demand after retention may admit one recovery owner. S07's expired result is shared for the same retention; `Pending`, `Expired`, `Failed` and `Busy` map to the existing public world error class. A world-local unwind conversion may publish an ordinary failed result so a panicking store cannot produce an immediate same-cell retry wave; no generic S07 change is allowed. Runtime shutdown may cancel owned async work as before, without producing a successful empty restore.

## Aggregate store policy

**WC3 — explicit finite policy.** Existing 21 partitions, body caps, S06 per-operation bounds and fanout 4 are retained. Add `WorldLoadLimits` to `CatalogWorldStore` with aggregate raw payload bytes and fetch-count limits. Production defaults are 32MiB and 2048 fetches, respectively: the existing S06 default single-operation `output_bytes` and `entries` bounds applied to the whole world load. These are conservative admission choices, not production-capacity measurements or a promise to accept every history previously within 21 separate S06 bounds. No environment/configuration flag is added.

Use checked arithmetic, accept raw_bytes in 1..=32MiB and fetches in 1..=2048, reject zero/overflow/out-of-range limits at construction, and allow small injected valid limits for discriminating tests. The byte counter sums every retained `RawFetch.body.len()` and the count includes every retained fetch, including equal identities; no silent deduplication/truncation or partial replay changes ordering. A partition must also stop constructing its `RawFetch` output if the per-load fetch/byte policy would already be exceeded. On aggregate exceedance return `StoreError::Storage(Error::Capacity)` and discard the entire load. `World` maps it to the existing error path with 0 VC calls.

**WC4 — include in-flight and completed unconsumed outputs.** Use a rolling JoinSet of at most 4 scheduled partition tasks. A slot is replenished only after a completed result is joined, checked against aggregate limits and retained or discarded. A task's reservation therefore covers its network/read result and its completed-but-unconsumed output. Do not spawn all 21 tasks behind a semaphore whose permit is released before result consumption. Preserve result sorting by `(fetched_at_ms, sha256)` after complete success.

A world partition reservation is the existing S06 `output_bytes` allowance (default 32MiB) and a bounded fetch-output allowance (at most the configured 2048 fetches). At defaults, the separately declared logical raw allowance is `32MiB retained + 4 × 32MiB in flight = 160MiB`, with at most 2048 retained fetches plus 4 × 2048 reserved output fetch slots. The conservative allowance may double-count a just-joined slot; it is intentionally safe. It is not an RSS bound: S06 control objects, compressed buffers, started storage blocking jobs, parsed/derived values, Arc metadata and multiple concurrent cells retain their existing separate limits. No process-wide cold-owner, shared CPU or S3-class budget is introduced here.

Apply the same aggregate byte/fetch policy and bounded accumulation to `load_first_dates`; its sequential partition scheduling stays sequential. Preserve its strict excluded-group behavior. Any summary caller newly refused by the cap remains a failed/incomplete summary input, never a successful truncated history.

## Compatibility and acceptance

- **WC5:** Existing cold oracle/weather/widget bytes, day/DST and range selection, no-yesterday markers, exact cell privacy, publication acknowledgement and provider backoff remain. The `WorldStore` trait and existing `CatalogWorldStore::new` callers remain source-compatible; explicit limits are available through a new constructor. No normalized weather persistence, provider timer or implicit stale-success policy is added.
- **WC6:** Proposed checks must demonstrate 16 same-cell waiters share one storage failure, 0 provider calls, completed-error suppression, recovery after retention, initiating-waiter cancellation and independent-cell progress. They must also cover owner expiry without deadline restart, idle trim/recreation, tiny aggregate byte/count overflows, under-budget exact sorting, at most 4 outstanding results including unconsumed completions, early abort cleanup and `load_first_dates` behavior. Test designs are not Red/Green evidence.
- **WC7:** After root implementation approval: retain an intended failing-before public behavior check, candidate-bound scoped and neighboring regressions, an additional actual owned-HTTP executable world smoke, exact placement/artifact checks, root-owned architecture/Archify updates, eligible independent review and exact CI. No pre-merge or production-readiness claim is possible from this preparation. Preserve baseline failures and do not weaken deadlines/assertions to make this slice pass.
