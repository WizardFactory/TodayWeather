# Acceptance for each stored life-index content once

Tests named `distinct_tests::*` are lib tests (`src/domestic/kma/source/life_distinct_tests.rs`, a child module of `life_input`, which reads the fold's applied set and last applied acquisition); `life_s3::*` is in `tests/domestic.rs`. All run on a paused clock over an isolated in-memory S3 with provider doubles; no provider or AWS is reached.

- **READ:** `Reader::distinct` returns, per partition, what `Reader::acquisitions_counted` (S06 `lookup`) returns: the same acquisitions in the same order, with their completions, partitions, pages and raw ids, and the same exclusions. A content whose first group is lost is read from the next one, which is counted; a different content between them keeps its place and applies first. A corrupt member of the group a content is read from fails the read with the same error as before; a partition whose every group is excluded fails with `incomplete catalog`, and so does an absent catalog (never published, listing no acquisition, or listed with its object gone), as `lookup` does. Checked by `distinct_tests::the_life_read_folds_as_every_group_restored`, `distinct_tests::a_content_whose_first_acquisition_is_lost_is_read_from_the_next`, `distinct_tests::a_corrupt_or_wholly_lost_partition_fails_the_read_as_before` and `distinct_tests::an_absent_catalog_fails_the_read_as_before`.
- **EQUALITY:** the committed fold (rows), each kind's last issuance, the pollen day+3, the applied set and the last applied acquisition equal an oracle that restores every group through `Reader::all` and folds them in the read's order, after the initial read (needed-first and full), an incremental read and a full re-read. The partitions hold 1, 5 then 6, and 30 identical UV acquisitions, distinct issuances before and after them, four contents completing together (two of them sharing the first page's fetch instant), a weeds pollen kind with day+3, UV acquisitions completing exactly at the 10-day `remove_old` edge and 1 ms after it, and an older issuance completing after a newer one. Checked by `distinct_tests::the_life_read_folds_as_every_group_restored`. The stated difference (a later copy whose own descriptor or page is lost is applied with the restored pages at its own completion; before, it was excluded) by `distinct_tests::a_later_acquisition_whose_own_objects_are_lost_is_applied_with_the_read_pages`.
- **COST:** the first read of today's UV partition holding 1, 10 or 50 identical 4-page acquisitions makes 2 catalog GETs, 1 descriptor GET and 4 body GETs each time (before: 10 descriptors and 40 bodies with 10). Checked by `distinct_tests::identical_acquisitions_cost_the_first_read_nothing`. A restart over 20 identical 4-page acquisitions of today's UV slot, the slot before it and two issuances of each pollen kind, every GET and LIST taking 40 ms behind the shared admission of 12, answers today's UV without asking the UV provider within the 700 ms head start, and the next request makes no S3 request (before: UV asked once, the first request took 2.5 s). Checked by `life_s3::a_restart_over_identical_uv_acquisitions_reads_before_the_head_start`.
- **PUBLISH:** a UV acquisition whose content another host stored is not published (no PUT, no own publication), its outcome is `Stored` with the stored group's partition and completion, and the request that waited answers from its records. Another content, no catalog, the matching group's descriptor lost, or its page lost: published. Checked by `distinct_tests::an_identical_acquisition_is_not_published_and_answers_at_once` and `distinct_tests::an_acquisition_without_a_restorable_twin_is_published`.
- **BUDGET:** the provider answers 1 ms before the end of its time and the check's catalog read stalls: the check gives up at 3 s and the acquisition is published within the owned time. UV slots of one flight (owned 600 s): with the 12:00 and 09:00 slots answering no data after 290 s each, the 06:00 slot completing at 590 s is published without a check although S3 holds its content; completing at 585 s it is checked and not published. An attempt's provider deadline stays 480 s from its start for owner deadlines of 493 s and 600 s; a UV or pollen request is owned 493 s, KASI and MFDS 20 s as before. Checked by `distinct_tests::a_stalled_check_is_published_within_the_owned_time`, `distinct_tests::a_candidate_completing_near_the_owned_cap_is_published_without_a_check` and `distinct_tests::an_attempt_keeps_the_provider_time_of_before`.
- **OVERLAY:** the `Stored` acquisition's entry carries the stored group's key; the next request within the memo makes no life LIST; the entry leaves when a read applies the stored group. A refresh that listed before another host stored the group and commits after the acquisition keeps the entry (design review DL-stored-overlay), and the needed-first fold of a first read, though it holds the stored group, does not take it out before the read commits. Checked by `distinct_tests::an_identical_acquisition_is_not_published_and_answers_at_once`, `distinct_tests::a_refresh_without_the_stored_group_keeps_the_identical_acquisition` and `distinct_tests::the_needed_first_fold_does_not_take_an_identical_acquisition_out`.
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-LIFE-DISTINCT.json` (base: master), and the artifact policy passes on the outgoing commits.

Failure paths: a failed or late check publishes (fail open); a failed read keeps the last committed fold and is tried again by the next request.
