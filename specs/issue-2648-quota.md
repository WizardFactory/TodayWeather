## Revision 2: bounded current-run lifecycle
Add GATHER_CURRENT_DEADLINE_MS, default540000 (nine minutes, shorter than the ten-minute poll interval), validated as positive integer within timer bounds. The deadline covers coordinates, coverage, fetch, retry and save/readback phases. On deadline, mark the run cancelled, abort its active HTTP transports, clear its scheduled recursive retry, stop admitting save work and complete joined callers once with a sanitized deadline error. Release only the owning run; late coordinates, collection and readback callbacks cannot complete again, emit stale coverage or clear a newer run. A previously submitted Mongo read/write can finish later; no transport cancellation or rollback is claimed. Late save callback cannot admit the next row or retry. Different-publication busy remains eligible at a later poll, with no mandatory queue. Other products do not use this current-run control.
Telemetry emits current-collection-stop with UTC time/publication and static deadline reason. This records termination, not recovered coverage or measured entitlement. Partial provider observations remain repairable under the existing8field completeness contract.
