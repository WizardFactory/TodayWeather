# Acceptance for the restart allocator and the accept clock

- **ALLOCATOR:** heap allocations, small and large, on the calling thread and on another thread, lie in mimalloc's heap region. `src/lib.rs` declares `mimalloc` 0.1.52 (`libmimalloc-sys` 0.1.49, mimalloc 3.3.2; MIT) with its defaults as the global allocator of the library every binary, example and test links. Checked by `allocator_tests::heap_allocations_come_from_mimalloc` (`src/lib.rs`, with the dev-dependency `libmimalloc-sys` and its `extended` feature). CI's Linux job builds the static musl release binary.
- **ACCEPT-CLOCK:** through the gateway, a native backend's attempt ends at the earlier of the gateway deadline and the accept instant plus the backend's budget. With a geocoder that answers after 300 ms, station providers that never answer and an empty in-memory store on a paused clock, the observed answer is sent 2.5 s after the accept (2.8 s before the change). Checked by `the_observed_wait_counts_from_the_accept_of_the_request` (`tests/domestic/full_response.rs`). A direct caller of `KmaBackend::outcome` keeps its bound from the call.
- **MEASURED:** EC2, 2026-11-10, t3.small, one bucket filled for 55 minutes by master, 14 restarts in ABBA order, #2907 (43d02167) against this change (aa7e2e9d). First answer from accept to response: 3,327 to 5,640 ms with 2 of 7 answering 501, against 2,550 to 2,616 ms with none. Median first-answer assembly: 643 ms against 65 ms. Malloc-lock share of the CPU samples: 54 to 55% against 0%. Median peak RSS in the first 60 s: 192 MB against 300 MB. Evidence: `reports/kma-ec2-20261110a/summary.json` (local, not tracked).
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-RESTART-ALLOC.json` (base `origin/feat/2614-kma-restart-cpu`), and the artifact policy passes on the outgoing commits.

Failure paths: a first answer after a restart can still be a 501 when a stored read fails (decision 3: unchanged in this round); PR-Z removes the forecast-zone history read that caused the measured ones. The memory envelope over a longer window is part of C.
