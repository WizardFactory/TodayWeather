# Acceptance for the station product after a fill and the early fold

Tests run over the S06 loopback S3 test peer with provider doubles; no provider or AWS is reached. Their order is set by gates (held S3 reads, a full bulk lane, held provider answers), and later instants are injected as the request's `now`. Red was recorded on the unchanged code before the change.

- **FLUSH:** a raced restart stores today's product while its fill pass still waits for the four hours S3 does not list. When the pass ends with them, the day's product is stored again with no request and no fold of the filled pages in between. The next restart reads it, restores no station page of the day and answers as the first process (`the_end_of_a_fill_pass_stores_the_filled_days_product`; Red: no product with the filled hours, and the next restart restored 4 pages one by one; Green: 0). A product worker that waits on a full bulk lane when the pass ends stores the filled day's product once the lane frees (`the_end_of_a_fill_pass_reaches_a_running_product_worker`; Red: the day's only product lacked 05:00 to 08:00). The end of a pass lets a changed day pass the ten-minute gate once. A day whose made-of is unchanged is not stored, and the flush is spent; a day whose read failed, or a process that has not folded yet, stores nothing and keeps the flush (`the_intent_of_a_fill_pass_bypasses_only_the_ten_minute_gate`, unit test in `observe.rs`; Red: did not compile without the set of flushed days). Two processes store one day's products with different pages in reverse order of their pages: B stores the 11:00 pages it fetched at 11:19, then A stores the same hours fetched at 11:18. A restart reads A's product, restores B's two later pages from the catalogs and keeps them. Its answers, page identities and the day's `knownIds` (A's two pages) and `excludedIds` equal a cold read of the same raw pages (`two_processes_products_in_reverse_order_keep_the_newest_pages`, which passes before and after the change).
- **EARLY-FOLD:** with the minute pages of the restart read held, and separately with the 09-20 product read held, the first answer has station 108's hourly rows with `rs1h` (answered as `rn1`) for every hour of today and yesterday, 35 of 35. Once the held read ends, the settled answers equal the producing process's. Checked by `todays_and_yesterdays_pages_are_folded_before_the_minute_pages` and `an_older_days_slow_product_does_not_hold_back_today_and_yesterday` (Red: 0 of 35 for both).
- **S16:** both early-fold tests assert that the first answer is not complete while the read runs. The existing restart-read, product, summary and full-response suites pass unchanged (`tests/domestic/observe.rs`, `observe_product.rs`, `summary_s3.rs`, `full_response.rs`), including the unsettled rest of the read, the excluded group kept partial and the repaired group completing its day.
- **RESIDUAL** (DR-V-residual-lag; the cost of each case, in targeted restores of the next restart):
  - A process that stored its products acquires the 11:00 AWS and city pages at 11:20. Its product is not stored again within ten minutes, so a quick restart restores 2 pages and stores the day's product; the restart after it restores 0 (`a_quick_restart_after_a_request_time_acquisition_restores_its_pages`).
  - A process stops in the middle of its fill pass: three hours are acquired and 05:00 never answers. The next restart restores the 3 pages the pass acquired (`a_restart_in_the_middle_of_a_fill_restores_what_it_acquired`).
  - A process stops after its pass made the four pages durable but before the pass ends. The next restart restores 4 pages and stores the day's product; the restart after it restores 0 (`a_stop_before_the_end_of_a_fill_costs_the_next_restart_its_pages`). For comparison, b1 restored 23 pages one by one.

  These pass before and after the change: they characterize the remaining lag, which the change does not remove.
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-STATION-FLUSH.json` (base `aa7e2e9d`), and the artifact policy passes on the outgoing commits.

Failure paths:

- A product store that fails spends the flush, and the ten-minute gate applies again.
- A pass cut by its owned deadline (600 s) or stopped by a crash stores nothing at its end (RESIDUAL).
- The early fold waits for a fold already running, which may have taken its pages before today's comparison ended, and then folds.
- Today's and yesterday's products are checked against a city set that does not yet include the older days' city pages (seed, held pages and their own products). If only an older day's city page names a city station, the existing read of the grown city set (`Flight::Expand`) reads today's reduced pages in full again. A product stored before that read covers the smaller set. A restart with the larger set then reads that day's raw pages, as it does for any product whose city set is too small.
