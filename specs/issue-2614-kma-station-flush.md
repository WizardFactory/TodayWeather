# Acceptance for the station product after a fill and the early fold

Integration tests run over the S06 loopback S3 test peer with provider doubles; no provider or AWS is reached. Their order is set by gates (held S3 reads, a full bulk lane, held provider answers), and later instants are injected as the request's `now`. Time contracts are checked on a paused clock over an isolated in-memory S3, where virtual time advances only when every task waits for a timer (unit tests in `server2/src/domestic/kma/source/observe_flush_tests.rs`). Red was recorded on the code before each change.

- **FLUSH:**
  - A raced restart stores today's product while its fill pass still waits for the four hours S3 does not list. When the pass ends with them, the day's product is stored again with no request and no fold of the filled pages in between. The next restart reads it, restores no station page of the day and answers as the first process (`the_end_of_a_fill_pass_stores_the_filled_days_product`; Red: no product with the filled hours, and the next restart restored 4 pages one by one; Green: 0).
  - A product worker that waits on a full bulk lane when the pass ends stores the filled day's product once the lane frees (`the_end_of_a_fill_pass_reaches_a_running_product_worker`; Red: the day's only product lacked 05:00 to 08:00).
  - The end of a pass lets a changed day pass the ten-minute gate once. A day whose made-of is unchanged is not stored, and the flush is spent; a day whose read failed, or a process that has not folded yet, stores nothing and keeps the flush (`the_intent_of_a_fill_pass_bypasses_only_the_ten_minute_gate`, unit test in `observe.rs`; Red: did not compile without the set of flushed days).
  - A reload's catalog read of today fails, and a fill pass then ends with the 06:00 page. The worker keeps the flush, and the fold of the page asks it again in vain. The next reload finds every listed page of the day held, so no page changes and the hourly part is not folded again; it makes the day readable, and that read alone has the day's product stored with the filled page (`a_flush_kept_by_a_failed_day_read_is_stored_after_the_next_read`, paused clock; code review 1, SF-flush-read-recovery; Red: 1 product of the day where 2 were expected).
  - Two processes store one day's products with different pages in reverse order of their pages: B stores the 11:00 pages it fetched at 11:19, then A stores the same hours fetched at 11:18. A restart reads A's product, restores B's two later pages from the catalogs and keeps them. Its answers, page identities and the day's `knownIds` (A's two pages) and `excludedIds` equal a cold read of the same raw pages (`two_processes_products_in_reverse_order_keep_the_newest_pages`, which passes before and after the change).
- **EARLY-FOLD:**
  - Time contract, on a paused clock (code review 1, SF-real-clock-deadlines): with the minute pages of a restart's read held past the request's wait, and separately with the 09-20 product read held, the first answer comes when its 2.5 s wait ends and has station 108's hourly rows with `rs1h` (answered as `rn1`) for every hour of today and yesterday, 35 of 35 (`the_first_answer_has_today_and_yesterday_while_the_minute_pages_are_held`, `the_first_answer_has_today_and_yesterday_while_an_older_days_product_is_held`; Red with the read order before the change: 0 of 35 for both).
  - Over the S3 peer, order and final result only: the held minute pages, or the held 09-20 product, are reached during the first answer, and once they arrive the settled answers equal the producing process's (`held_minute_pages_leave_the_first_answer_incomplete_until_they_arrive`, `a_held_older_day_product_leaves_the_first_answer_incomplete_until_it_arrives`).
  - A city station that only an older day's city page names (강남, 400, at 09-20 08:00), with the newest product of every day made for the whole city set. A restart whose 09-20 product read is held folds today and yesterday first for the smaller set. Once the read ends, it reads today's and yesterday's AWS pages in full again (24 and 11 on the bulk lane), and its settled answers, values and page identities, equal a cold read of the same raw pages. It stores those two days' products again for the smaller set (`a_city_station_only_an_older_day_names_is_read_in_full_after_the_early_fold`; code review 1, SF-older-city-set-test).
- **S16:** the early-fold tests, on the paused clock and over the S3 peer, assert that the first answer is not complete while the read runs. The existing restart-read, product, summary and full-response suites pass unchanged (`tests/domestic/observe.rs`, `observe_product.rs`, `summary_s3.rs`, `full_response.rs`), including the unsettled rest of the read, the excluded group kept partial and the repaired group completing its day.
- **RESIDUAL** (DR-V-residual-lag; the cost of each case, in targeted restores of the next restart):
  - A process that stored its products acquires the 11:00 AWS and city pages at 11:20. Its product is not stored again within ten minutes, so a quick restart restores 2 pages and stores the day's product; the restart after it restores 0 (`a_quick_restart_after_a_request_time_acquisition_restores_its_pages`).
  - A process stops in the middle of its fill pass: three hours are acquired and 05:00 never answers. The next restart restores the 3 pages the pass acquired (`a_restart_in_the_middle_of_a_fill_restores_what_it_acquired`).
  - A process stops after its pass made the four pages durable but before the pass ends. The next restart restores 4 pages and stores the day's product; the restart after it restores 0 (`a_stop_before_the_end_of_a_fill_costs_the_next_restart_its_pages`). For comparison, b1 restored 23 pages one by one.

  These pass before and after the change: they characterize the remaining lag, which the change does not remove.
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-STATION-FLUSH.json` with the merge-base with the PR-A branch as its base (`git merge-base HEAD origin/measure/2614-kma-alloc-clock`), and the artifact policy passes on the outgoing commits.

Failure paths:

- A product store that fails spends the flush, and the ten-minute gate applies again.
- A pass cut by its owned deadline (600 s) or stopped by a crash stores nothing at its end (RESIDUAL).
- The early fold waits for a fold already running, which may have taken its pages before today's comparison ended, and then folds.
- Today's and yesterday's products are read beside the older days' products. Whichever is applied first decides the city set (seed, held pages and products) the other is checked against. While only an older day's city page names a city station:
  - Today's and yesterday's products are usually applied first, and always while an older day's product read is slow. They are then accepted and folded for the smaller set. Once the older days' products arrive, the read of the grown city set (`Flight::Expand`) reads today's and yesterday's AWS pages in full again (35 at 10:50, on the bulk lane after the first answer). Products made for the whole set are stored again for the smaller set.
  - When the older days' products are applied first, today's and yesterday's products made for the smaller set are refused, and those days' raw pages are read before the answer, as before this change.
  - Answers settle equal to a cold read either way.

  Before this change a restart applied every product before its first fold. Once one restart had stored the products for the whole set, later restarts had no such cost. A process that reads the raw pages had this order before the change too, because it reads its older days after the first fold.
