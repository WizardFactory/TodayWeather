# Acceptance for empty and partly stored KMA requests

- **EMPTY:** with an empty store and the observed source, `forecast-zone`, `mid-ta`, `kma-stn-info`, `airkorea-station-list`, `kasi-area-rise-set` and `pwn-cd` are each listed once and no `pwn-cd` object is read, and the first response has 18 daily rows through D+10 with `dailyStatus.healthy` true. Every row of the region table resolves to a collected land zone before any list is stored. Checked by `tests/domestic/full_response.rs` and `tests/domestic/towns.rs`.
- **PARTIAL:** under the observed source, a latest publication the listing shows absent is requested while stored reads run, and a listed publication that proves unusable is still eligible afterwards. Checked by `tests/domestic/full_response.rs`.
- **WINDOW:** the observed source reads no stored grid publication older than 54 hours, and stops at a region's newest mid publication with a document. `S3Source::new` keeps its window and its empty zone list, so the legacy oracle tests hold. The policy identity ends with `stored-read-window-v1`. Checked by `tests/domestic/full_response.rs`, `source_s3_oracle.rs` and `observed_history.rs`.
- **WARNINGS:** a stored zone state restores entries and order, later days replay on top, older days are not read, and a seed imported later voids it. An empty store reads no `pwn-cd` object. A day with only raw objects is still repaired. The collector stores the state once a KST day, only after its sixty-day bootstrap, and a failed store does not fail the cycle. Checked by `tests/warnings/recovery.rs` and `collector.rs`.
- **KASI:** a request waits at most 500 ms for the restore, which continues on its own, and a later request has the result. A capture pass stores one fold; a new process restores from it with no read of an area's day and no listing of the kind when the fold is at most a day old, and an older fold is completed from the listing. Checked by `tests/domestic/life_s3.rs`.
- **CHECK:** `kma_full_response_contract.py` reports unhealthy `dailyStatus`, dates missing through D+10, a short list that ends before the end of D+2 and holes in it, and exits non-zero. Checked by `tools/verification/test_kma_full_response_contract.py`.
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against the single declaration, and the artifact policy passes on outgoing commits.

Failure paths: an unreadable fold or stored state falls back to the original objects. A failed KASI listing or read leaves the earlier fold and is retried. A provider that fails leaves the existing omit-late behavior.
