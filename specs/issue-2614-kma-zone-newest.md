# Acceptance for forecast zones from the newest stored list

Tests named `zone_select::*`, `source::*` and `source_s3_oracle::*` are in `tests/domestic.rs`; `zone_retry_tests::*` are lib tests (`src/domestic/kma/source/zone_retry_tests.rs`). All run on a paused clock with an injected wall clock, over an isolated in-memory store, except the S3 HTTP oracle and the S15b1 regressions, which use the loopback S3 peer.

- **SELECT:** `S3Source::zones` answers from the newest eligible list: candidates from `Reader::partitions` and `Reader::listed_groups` (catalog control objects only), the anchor day and its previous day, one group restored by `Reader::members` per try, and the restored group's identity equal to its catalog entries' (otherwise a read error). Checked by `zone_select::an_incomplete_newest_day_falls_back_to_the_list_that_completed_last` (design review 3: C incomplete on 11-10, B 11-09 00:00:00 to 00:00:01, A requested 11-08 23:59:59 and completed 11-09 00:00:05: A answers) and `zone_select::a_restored_list_must_equal_its_catalog_entries`.
- **ORDER:** the order key is (completion instant, partition, digest of the page hashes in page order) from the catalog entries. Checked by `zone_select::interleaved_page_times_order_lists_by_their_last_page` (pages at 100 and 400 s beat pages at 200 and 300 s), `zone_select::a_list_completed_after_midnight_answers_when_it_completed_last` and `zone_select::equal_completions_order_by_the_digest_of_the_pages`.
- **FALLBACK:** an S06-incomplete newest group and one legacy would not have written are skipped, a list completed after the request's instant is not selected, nothing eligible gives the land zones (observed) or an empty collection (legacy) without identity, and a corrupt newest member stays a `forecast-zone read` error with an older list present. Checked by `zone_select::an_incomplete_newest_day_falls_back_to_the_list_that_completed_last`, `zone_select::a_newest_list_legacy_would_not_write_is_skipped`, `zone_select::a_list_completed_after_now_is_not_selected`, `zone_select::a_corrupt_newest_list_keeps_the_read_error` and `zone_retry_tests::an_empty_store_answers_packaged_zones_then_the_acquired_list`.
- **INSTALL:** installs happen under the zones lock and only for a newer identity; a failed acquisition keeps the last good list; a check that re-confirms the installed list moves the check time, so the next check comes 10 minutes later. Checked by `zone_retry_tests::an_older_result_never_replaces_a_newer_installed_list`, `zone_retry_tests::a_failed_acquisition_keeps_the_last_good_list`, `zone_retry_tests::a_reconfirmed_list_moves_the_check_time` and `source_s3_oracle::a_late_zone_list_completed_earlier_does_not_replace_the_newer_one` (replaces S15b1-R7).
- **FRESH:** a restart with a list completed an hour ago acquires nothing; a list exactly 24 h old is not acquired and one 24 h and 1 ms old is; a long-running process acquires again 24 h after its last list; a failure is retried after the five-minute admission and absent, panicked, empty, malformed or incomplete results install nothing; requests do not wait for the owner, whose list is installed when it ends; an empty store answers the land zones and then the acquired list, before S3 holds it. Checked by `zone_retry_tests::a_restart_with_a_fresh_stored_list_acquires_nothing`, `zone_retry_tests::the_list_is_acquired_once_it_completed_more_than_24_hours_ago`, `zone_retry_tests::a_long_running_process_acquires_again_after_24_hours`, `zone_retry_tests::failed_zone_collection_retries_after_existing_memo`, `zone_retry_tests::absent_panicked_and_unreadable_results_remain_retryable`, `zone_retry_tests::requests_return_while_one_owner_continues_and_installs` and `zone_retry_tests::an_empty_store_answers_packaged_zones_then_the_acquired_list`.
- **ORACLE:** the five legacy zone runs of `fixtures/source.json.gz` (34 queries) give legacy's `regId`s and result counts and the same `collected` judgement for every land zone through the selector. Checked by `source::forecast_zones_match_legacy_get_from_kma` (in-memory store) and `source_s3_oracle::forecast_zones_read_from_s3_equal_the_legacy_oracle` (S3 HTTP peer); `source_s3_oracle::forecast_zone_fields_come_from_the_newest_list_alone` replaces S15b1-R5 (a field only an older list set is not kept).
- **COST:** a cold read over a newest day of 3 lists and 10, then 20, older days of 5 identical lists each makes 1 LIST, 3 catalog GETs, 1 descriptor GET and 1 raw GET both times (before the change: 1, 11, 53 and 53 with 10 older days). Checked by `zone_select::reads_do_not_grow_with_identical_lists_in_older_days`.
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-ZONE-NEWEST.json` (base: the PR-A commit), and the artifact policy passes on the outgoing commits.

Failure paths: a failed check returns the read error and keeps memory and its check time, so the next request checks again; a failed acquisition keeps memory and retries after five minutes.
