# Acceptance for station observations after a restart

- **MINUTE:** `CatalogStore::lookup_members` reads one catalog and then only the named members' groups: one descriptor and one raw body per member. An unlisted member answers incomplete, a member of another namespace is refused, and the store deadline holds. A restart over 200 stored minute groups restores at most the 20 within reach, with the minute catalogs read a bounded number of times. Checked by `targeted_members_read_one_catalog_and_only_their_groups` (`tests/storage/catalog_wire.rs`) and `a_restart_restores_only_the_minute_pages_within_reach` (`tests/domestic/observe.rs`).
- **PRODUCT:** a product keeps every stored value and key order, each distinct registry facts row once, and refuses another day, schema or parse revision. A folding process stores one product per day whose hours are all in the window and stores none again while nothing changed. Checked by `every_value_and_key_order_round_trips` (unit test in `observe_product.rs`) and `a_restart_answers_from_the_day_products`.
- **RESTART:** a restarted process with no provider answers the 168 past hourly pages and minute rows on its first request, within the bound. It reads no raw page that the products cover, stores no product and answers as the producing process. A page published after the product is restored by itself and the answers equal a cold read of the raw pages. Of two processes' products the newest is read. Checked by `a_restart_answers_from_the_day_products`, `a_page_published_after_the_product_is_restored` and `the_newest_of_two_processes_products_is_read` (`tests/domestic/observe_product.rs`).
- **FALLBACK:** an unreadable product, an older schema, an older parse revision and a grown city set each give that day's raw pages; a page the catalog does not list is dropped and its hour restored. Each answers as the raw pages do and stores the day's product again. Checked by `unusable_products_are_replaced_by_the_raw_pages`.
- **ACCOUNTED** (review RO-city-growth, RO-minute-fallback, RO-sticky-excluded): a city page published after the products that names another station makes the restart read the reduced AWS pages in full again, and its answers equal a cold raw read. A minute whose newest acquisition S06 excludes is read from the next acquisition of that minute, as a cold read gives it. A group excluded when the product was made and repaired later completes its day on a restart: the answers equal a cold read, they settle, the day's product is stored again without the exclusion, and a new process records the daily summaries. While those pages cannot be read in full the answers stay unsettled, and a later request tries again without a new hourly page; a read slower than one run's limit keeps what it read and finishes over later runs. Checked by `a_station_joining_the_city_set_after_the_products_is_read_in_full`, `a_failed_full_read_keeps_answers_unsettled_until_it_is_tried_again`, `a_slow_full_read_progresses_over_several_runs`, `an_incomplete_newest_minute_acquisition_falls_back_to_an_older_one`, `a_repaired_group_completes_its_day_after_the_product` (`tests/domestic/observe_product.rs`) and `a_repaired_station_group_lets_a_restart_record_summaries` (`tests/domestic/summary_s3.rs`).
- **S16:** answers are unsettled while the rest of the restart read runs and settle after it. A day read with an S06-excluded group stays partial through its product (`excluded` 1); a restart checks only that group again and reads no other raw page of the day. The station page products are not cached on disk. Checked by `the_rest_of_the_restart_read_keeps_answers_unsettled`, `an_excluded_group_keeps_its_day_partial_through_the_product` and `the_station_page_product_is_not_cached` (`tests/cache.rs`); the existing `tests/domestic/observe.rs` cases keep the legacy answers, and the `tests/domestic/summary_s3.rs` cases record their summaries from the first complete answer after a start.
- **RESPONSE-FIRST:** a response's own raw groups are published only after its body ends, also when a provider is done before its caller is polled again or a waiter returns before the writer counts it. Checked under CPU saturation by `a_creating_waiter_holds_its_instant_provider_until_the_body_ends` (`tests/response_first.rs`; it failed 17 of 20 runs before the change) and by `empty_weather_store_serves_seven_days_before_raw_publication_and_replays_identically` (`tests/domestic/full_response.rs`, failed in CI run 42379684133).
- **PLACEMENT:** the placement gate passes against `server2/config/tasks/KMA-RESTART-OBSERVE.json` (base `origin/feat/2614-kma-restart-reads`), and the artifact policy passes on outgoing commits.

A raw key with out-of-range numbers is not a record identity (`absurd_record_keys_are_refused`, `tests/storage/codec.rs`).

Failure paths: a day whose catalogs cannot be listed or whose raw read fails stays failed (S16) and stores no product. A product that cannot be stored is tried again after ten minutes. A newer page that is not complete or does not parse gives the day's raw read.
