# Plan for no provider race during the first life read

Task declaration: `server2/config/tasks/KMA-LIFE-FIRST-READ.json`. Based on master 9f078ad3, after #2927 (KMA-LIFE-DISTINCT). Design: revision 2 of `reports/sdlc/issue-2614-kma-life-first-read/design.md` (not tracked), approved after two design reviews.

1. Tests first, against the unchanged `life()`: the in-crate T1, T2, T3, T6, T7 and T8 and the T9 forced-slow and rewritten restart tests fail with UV and pollen calls during the first read; T4, T5, the unchanged KMA-LIFE-DISTINCT restart test and the NEEDED-FIRST bound pass (behaviour kept).
2. `LifeInputs`: `first_read_started` and `first_read_ended`; the start callback of the life read's owned work creates `FirstReadEnd` for the first owned read and moves it into the work; `life()` pushes no UV or pollen flight while `first_read_ended` is false.
3. Guards: temporary mutations of the guard, the gate condition, waiter outcomes, later reads, the interim fold and the family listing are each caught by a test.
4. Documents: the S15b-3b life-index input (rule, bounds, stated differences), the restart race (life row, quota note, verification) and the restart acceptance link in `docs/architecture/server2.md`. No Archify diagram covers the server2 life read or the restart race; none is regenerated.
5. Local checks (format, clippy, placement, artifact policy, lib, domestic and workspace tests); cross-provider PR review and AK's merge decision follow.
