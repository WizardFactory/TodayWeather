# Regional data qualification plan

Use prior 48-city retrieval evidence and bounded official/public follow-up reads.
Replay the focus area and record counts and timings without embedding provider
payloads. Publish a shared register under docs/reference/regional-weather.

The main risk is implying live usability from an archive, a restriction-bound
endpoint or a reachable page without parseable status. Record exact response
scope and the known launch dependency per product. Verify internal navigation,
sample assertions, documented failures, and the artifact policy. A reader
checking a reachable sample should locate its request and geography. A reader
following a failed lookup should be able to identify the missing qualification.
