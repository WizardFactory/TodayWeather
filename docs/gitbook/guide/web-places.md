# Find, save and share a location on the web

Open **Locations** and enter a city or neighborhood in **Search locations**. Select a suggestion to save it. If no suggestions appear, enter at least two characters and choose **Search address** when available. In demo mode, choose a suggested location.

Choose **Current location** to look up the weather where you are. Allow access if your browser asks for permission. If access is blocked, check the site's browser permissions or search by name. The saved place stays the same when you travel.

On the weather screen, choose **Share location** to send a link to a suggested city's forecast. If you're asked to pick a city, choose one from the suggestions. The link doesn't include your exact coordinates. You can save up to 30 places. If the list is full, a suggestion may open without being saved. Remove an unused place to make room. Removing a place also removes its weather data from this browser.
