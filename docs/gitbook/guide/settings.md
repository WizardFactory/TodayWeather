# Change units, startup screen, refresh interval and theme

Open **☰** in the top-left corner of the weather screen.

| To change | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Celsius or Fahrenheit | Open **Units → Temperature Unit** and choose **C** or **F**. Return to the weather screen to see the change in the current temperature and chart. |
| Other units or air quality grades | In **Units**, select your wind speed, pressure, distance and precipitation units, or choose the air quality index standard. |
| The screen you see first | Under **Startup Page**, choose the hourly, daily, locations or air quality screen. Your choice applies the next time you open the app. |
| How often weather refreshes | Under **Refresh interval**, select a time interval or manual refresh. The interval applies while the app is open. To refresh manually, tap the selected weather tab again. |
| Colors and background | Under **Theme setting**, choose the light, dark, weather photo or old theme. The weather photo theme shows a background that matches the sky conditions. |
