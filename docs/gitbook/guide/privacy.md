# Manage usage analytics and ad privacy

Open **☰ → Optional usage analytics**. Choose **Allow** or **Decline / withdraw**, then check the status in the menu. **Off** means usage analytics is disabled. You can continue using the weather features either way.

Open **Ad privacy choices** to review the advertising options available in your region. Depending on your region and device, there may be no form to open.

On iPhone, tracking permission is a separate device setting. See [Change tracking permission on iPhone](ios-tracking.md).
