# Read weather bulletins for South Korea

When your device region is South Korea, open **☰ → Special weather**. Choose a weather warning, preliminary warning or weather information bulletin, then check the affected areas and publication time. A preliminary warning indicates that a warning may follow.

To compare temperatures, rainfall and wind across the country, open the [national maps](nation.md). If the list is empty or a bulletin looks old, check the latest Korea Meteorological Administration announcement.
