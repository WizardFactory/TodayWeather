# When location, weather updates or alerts stop working

**The app can't find my location.** Open **Locations** and search for your area by name. If that brings up the weather, check your device's location services and TodayWeather's location permission. On Android, if you've denied permission permanently, the settings button in the prompt can take you to app permissions.

**I removed all my locations.** Add a place to **Locations**, or tap **Edit** there and enable your current location. Check the place name at the top of the weather screen.

**The forecast hasn't changed.** Check the place name, update time and internet connection. Tap the selected weather tab again to reload. You can also retry from the weather error dialog. If only the air quality screen is empty, measurements may be unavailable for that area.

**My alarm didn't arrive.** Check notification permission in your phone settings. Open the bell for the relevant place, check the selected days and time, then tap **Save**. If the problem continues, [send feedback](share-help.md) with your app version, location and the time you checked.
