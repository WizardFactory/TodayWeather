# Read daily highs, lows and rain forecasts

Open **Daily**. Each column is one day. Read the morning and afternoon icons, the expected rain or snow amount, and the precipitation probability. The ends of the temperature bar show that day's low and high. The red dot on today's bar marks the current temperature.

Swipe right to see earlier dates or left to see the days ahead. Below the chart, **Weather details** shows the humidity, wind, precipitation probability, UV index, rainfall and snowfall supplied for each date. Tap the selected **Daily** tab again to refresh. In the air quality summary, tap a pollutant to open [air quality details](air.md).
