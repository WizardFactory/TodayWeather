# Switch or remove saved locations

Open **Locations** and tap a place name to see its weather. When you have several places saved, you can also tap the arrows at the edges of the weather screen or swipe **outside the chart** to move between places. Swiping inside the chart moves through time.

To tidy your list, tap **Locations → Edit**. Tap **⊗** beside a saved place to remove it, or use the switch beside your current location to show or hide it. Confirm your changes and check the list. The bell beside a place opens [its notification settings](alarms.md).
