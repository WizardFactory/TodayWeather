# Back up, restore or delete saved browser locations

Open **Settings → Back up locations → Export** to download your locations and settings. The export excludes your current location.

In another browser, choose **Import** and select the file. The confirmation shows how many locations you have now and how many are in the file. Continuing replaces your current list and settings. If you want to keep places from both lists, keep a separate record of your existing list first.

To remove everything, open **Browser data → Delete TodayWeather data in this browser** and confirm. This clears saved places, settings and stored weather. To remove just one place, use the delete control beside it under **Locations**.
