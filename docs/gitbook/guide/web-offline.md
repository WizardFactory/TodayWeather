# Add the web app to your home screen and use it offline

On iPhone or iPad, open the browser's share menu and choose **Add to Home Screen**. On Android or desktop, use the install option in your browser menu if available. You can also view the weather directly in your browser.

If the connection drops, you may see weather saved within the last 24 hours for places you've viewed. Check the observation time and forecast publication time to see how old the data is. If nothing was saved, reconnect and retry. If you see a request limit message, wait for the indicated time before refreshing.

When a new version is ready, finish any edits and choose **Update**. Once it's applied, choose **Reload** to open the new version. To fetch fresh weather for your place, choose **Refresh weather**.
