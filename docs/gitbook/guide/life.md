# Check weather details, UV and pollen risk

Scroll down in **Hourly** to **Weather details**. Depending on the location and available data, you can find humidity, wind, visibility, pressure, feels-like temperature, hourly rainfall, sunrise and sunset. Check the update time in the same table.

For a location in South Korea, look for the UV index and **Pollen risk**. The pollen summary shows the highest risk among the types reported for that day. Tap it to read the grade and precautions for each available type, such as oak or pine in spring, or weeds in late summer and autumn. Tap **Close** when you're done.

The pollen grade is a forecast based on weather conditions. **Low** is a reported grade. If the entire item is missing, there may be no usable data for that place and date.
