# Share weather, send feedback and find your app version

**Share the weather.** On Android, tap the share icon at the top right of the weather screen and select the receiving app. Check the message before sending. It includes the place, temperatures, a comparison with yesterday and an app link.

**Send feedback or write a review.** Open **☰ → Send feedback**. Include the location, screen and what happened so the team can identify the issue. **Rate this app** takes you to the store.

**Find your version and data sources.** Look for **Version** near the bottom of the menu. Open **About** to see weather and air quality sources. The privacy policy and terms of use are also available near the bottom.

To exit on Android, press your device's Back button from a main tab. Choose **OK** to exit or **Cancel** to stay.
