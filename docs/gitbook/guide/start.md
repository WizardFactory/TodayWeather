# See the weather for your area

When you first open TodayWeather on Android, read the access notice and tap **OK**. Then choose the area whose weather you want to see.

- To see the weather where you are, tap **Use your current location**. If Android asks for location permission, choose whether to allow it. The place name appears at the top of the weather screen.
- To look up a place yourself, type its name in **Enter city** and tap the matching result. You can also tap one of the city shortcuts on the start screen.

To keep another place handy, [add it to your locations](android-add-place.md). If you're using a browser, see [Find and save a location on the web](web-places.md).
