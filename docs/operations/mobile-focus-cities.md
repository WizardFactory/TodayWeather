# TodayWeather prelaunch focus cities

## Focus cities as of 2026-11-20

TodayWeather will focus on **48 metropolitan areas across all 21 priority countries**. South Korea has three focus cities: Seoul, Busan and Daegu. The first 45 earned their places by score across 19 countries, with no per-country quota. Lisbon and Montevideo brought Portugal and Uruguay into the [47-city selection on 2026-11-19](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/2613#issuecomment-7020036485). [Daegu was added on 2026-11-20](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/2613#issuecomment-7027717587) by explicit selection. This decision expands the [earlier six-city shortlist](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/2613#issuecomment-7019825060).

These are planning decisions. The issue records service availability, reliability and expected revenue as considerations. Public evidence did not establish a loss per additional user in every excluded market.

## Selection basis

The study assessed 59 metropolitan areas and ranked 58. The opportunity score weighted potential reach at 45%, national digital advertising spend per internet user at 35%, and the frequency of recurring weather conditions at 20%. It used public data available before launch. City-level revenue and profitability remain unverified. [Research method and sources](https://github.com/WizardFactory/TodayWeather/issues/2613#issuecomment-7019825060)
