# Sunshine, terrain, rainfall and coastal sources

[Delivery conventions](README.md).

| Area and product | Reproducible path | Usable data and remaining qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| London and Manchester, sunshine duration | [Open-Meteo forecast](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), forecast coordinates for each city, daily sunshine_duration and timezone Europe/London. | Evaluation returned all three local days (Nov 20–22) in seconds at both points. Keep local date and publish estimated duration. Met Office Heathrow monthly sunshine is a historical comparison. Manchester Ringway series ends in 2004. The [hosted commercial API](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing) requires subscription and a customer key. |
| Zürich and Vienna, freezing and snow levels; low cloud | [Open-Meteo forecast](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), hourly freezing_level_height and visibility. Use Europe/Zurich or Europe/Vienna local zone. Check regional sources at [MeteoSwiss forecast data](https://opendatadocs.meteoswiss.ch/e-forecast-data) and [GeoSphere Austria Data Hub](https://data.hub.geosphere.at/). | Evaluation returned 24 hourly freezing-level and visibility values in metres in each city on Nov 20. A 0 °C model height and point visibility are separate quantities from the snow/rain transition elevation and valley-cloud top. A verified height feed with mountain coverage and current update cadence is still needed for those products. Hosted commercial terms apply to Open-Meteo. |
| Recife, monitored rainfall and floods | [APAC ArcGIS station layer](https://geoportal.apac.pe.gov.br/server/rest/services/met_monitoramento_chuvas_pe/MapServer/0/query). Filter municipality Recife and request station name, coordinates, ultima_leitura_data_hora, hora_1, horas_3 and horas_24. | Confirmed named gauges and accumulated millimetres. Use observed timestamp and accumulation window. Confirm update delay and reuse rights. City Defesa Civil incident and road-control feeds are separately required for city impacts. |
| Bogotá, rain gauges and flood reports | [IDEAM precipitation archive](https://www.datos.gov.co/d/s54a-sgyg), municipality BOGOTA, D.C. [DHIME](https://www.ideam.gov.co/dhime) documents official locations and downloads. | Sample includes 300 historical ten-minute values from five gauges dated 2022-04-24/25. Both tested November date ranges returned no city data. Archive carries CC BY-SA 4.0. Qualify a current gauge feed and IDIGER official impact feed with reuse terms, including the distinction between measured rain and reported flooding. |
| Lisbon, coastal wave conditions | [IPMA oceanography](https://api.ipma.pt/), join daily significant wave height files to sea-location IDs. | Day-0 JSON and sea metadata returned. Keep publish and valid date, height unit, marine-location ID and coverage. Verify the coastal point selected for Lisbon and the product's re-use terms before integration. |
| Buenos Aires, observed and forecast estuary height | [SHN hourly port levels](https://www.hidro.gov.ar/oceanografia/AlturasHorarias.asp). Select Buenos Aires and retain the station, local date/time and vertical datum. | Historical port-level observations verified. A stable programmatic live observation and forecast product with confirmed runtime date, refresh interval, datum and redistribution remains pending. Use SHN for reported river levels and relevant warnings. |
| Montevideo, tides and measured levels | [SOHMA tide information](https://sohma.armada.mil.uy/index.php/servicios/informacion-mareografica) and [2026 official prediction table](https://sohma.armada.mil.uy/attachments/article/300/TABLA_DE_MAREAS_2026_MONTEVIDEO.pdf). | Parsed 8,760 hour-indexed predictions for the port and 2026 calendar. Use the stated tide datum, times and units. Current measured estuary level and warnings require separately verified issuer endpoints. Commercial redistribution terms for the tide product remain open. |
