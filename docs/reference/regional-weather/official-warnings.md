# Official weather warnings by viewed location

This source and response mapping covers the [48 focus cities in 21 markets](../../operations/mobile-focus-cities.md). It applies to server1 and the WebApp (PWA and desktop), native iOS and native Android. It records verified source behavior and the proposed delivery contract. The current KMA route remains available and each new issuer requires a verified location and lifecycle rule before its data is served.

## Server1 response and consumer contract

Use a separate GET under the public server1 coordinate gateway, `/weather/v000903/coord/{lat},{lon}/warnings`. Weather requests and their cache lifetime are independent. The lookup receives the viewed coordinate and passes the requested language to issuers when supported. Korea keeps the existing [KMA menu and weather warning paths](../../architecture/mobile-api.md#domestic-api-middleware-in-order). A later unified read can adapt KMA only after the warning-area and release cases pass.

- The envelope is `{location, checkedAt, sources, warnings}`. `location` carries the evaluated latitude, longitude and ISO country. `checkedAt` is the UTC lookup completion time. `sources` carries each expected issuer/product scope, `state` (`current`, `unavailable`, or `unsupported`), `asOf` and `freshUntil` when known. `current` requires an issuer response with current freshness and a confirmed point match rule. `unsupported` covers a source with no qualified territory/product. `unavailable` covers errors, stale data, ambiguous geography or unknown lifecycle. An empty `warnings` list means no warnings only for the scope of a successful, current source. A partial result may show validated warnings while displaying incomplete coverage for failed sources.
- A warning carries `id`, `issuer`, `issuerLabel`, `sourceUrl`, `eventCode` when defined, `event`, `product` (`warning`, `watch`, `advisory` or `statement`), `severity`, `urgency`, `certainty` when supplied, `language`, `headline`, `description` and `instruction` when supplied. Preserve the issuer's product and severity vocabulary. Text is rendered as text and the source link must point to an allowed issuer/relay. Translations remain variants of the same message.
- Keep separate UTC `sentAt`, `effectiveAt`, optional `onsetAt`, message `expiresAt` and optional hazard `endsAt`. Retain the issuer time zone as metadata when a source reports local time. Clients format display dates for the viewed location. A future onset can display as upcoming after the message becomes effective. Drop expired messages even if a stale source position still lists them. Where an explicit hazard end comes earlier than expiry, stop the in-force display at the earlier time. An outage past the last valid check is an unavailable coverage state.
- Establish product coverage by issuer point result, exact geometry or issuer area code; store `area` as the actual codes/names and `matchMethod` as `issuer-point`, `geometry` or `area-code`. Use GeoJSON longitude, latitude order. CAP polygons list latitude, longitude. Reject unknown geographic membership and retain the source state separately. A coastal adjacent point and a region-centroid approximation require the same precision as a downtown point.
- CAP stream identity uses `sender`, `identifier` and `sent`. Process actual public `Update` and `Cancel` references against that key before deciding visible geography. An updated message can move the area and remove a prior alert at the point. A cancelled or superseded message cannot reappear on a late delivery. For fresh authoritative point snapshots, replace the previous point list and apply timestamp/revision checks; an older snapshot must not replace a newer one. Simultaneous overlapping products remain separate unless issuer linkage is proven.
- On WebApp desktop/PWA and in both native repositories, key by the viewed location and discard a late result after a location change unless storing it under its original location. Enforce freshness and expiry at read/display time, including after device suspend. Offline weather snapshots and device country have no authority over official-alert status. Preserve the existing KMA readers for their original contract. Keep station air quality, modeled severe risk, SPC outlook polygons and NHC track/cone data outside this response.
