Start (K1) and evidence (K2) passed; the sample criterion (K3) was met by 38 of 56 families because of two flaws in the frozen policy, and the bill for K4 is not available yet. K3's shortfall comes from the policy, not from missing samples. A usable pair needs the same expected 2xx on both origins. Production answered all 14 conditional requests with 304 from CloudFront, so no conditional pair can be usable, and the candidate answered nine of them with 200. Production has no `/v000903/notice/latest` route and answered 404 seven times while the candidate answered 200; the policy expected 200. The evaluator therefore reports C3 `fail` (`unexpected_http_status`) and `no_go`. The week policy must set no minimum for conditional families. The notice difference cannot be expressed in the current policy format: the evaluator applies one `expected_statuses` list to both origins, so `[404]` makes the candidate's 200 unexpected and `[200, 404]` still leaves no usable pair. Judging notice needs per-origin expected statuses in the evaluator, or the case leaves the week schedule.

- **Nation.** All 14 nation answers were HTTP 200, where SR-6 saw HTTP 500 in 23 of 24 samples. An HTTP 200 shows the answer completed, not that every stored read did: the candidate logged 70 `s3/lookup` timeouts on the nation route, rising from 4–5 an hour at the start to 11–17 an hour at the end, each after 1.6–2.3 seconds; the route omits the late parts. Stored history keeps growing until its 54-hour window is full, so the nation p95 may pass the 8-second deadline early in a week; this is a projection from six hours, not an observation.
