## Simulator baseline, 18 November

Hourly chart pans and the first bundled place search are the two actions worth investigating on an iPhone. Horizontal chart motion repeatedly missed the simulator callback cadence. A search prototype reduced the component cost in both batches, but app-level timings varied enough to leave the user-visible improvement inconclusive. This investigation supplies a baseline for an optimization decision. Device frame rate and production speedup remain unverified. ## Scrolling, charts and iPad

The OS scroll signposts accumulated both forward and reverse gestures per iteration. Median drag duration was 550/550 ms and deceleration was 1,899/1,899 ms. The longest drag observation was 1,721 ms. A second build sampled `CADisplayLink` and UIKit offsets, retaining the scroll-view ancestor, dominant axis and touch release time. Each affected gesture has a moving interval longer than 1.5 times its callback budget. Excess is the sum of interval time above one budget for those flagged intervals. This approximates main-thread callback cadence. The composited frame times and actual hitch duration remain unavailable.
