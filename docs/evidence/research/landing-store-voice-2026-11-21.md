## Coverage

The Apple sample contains the first 50 distinct entries in the Korean public review feed sorted by most recent, spanning June 2021 through November 2026. Of these, 48 carry version 1.0.10 and two carry 1.1.x. All predate 2.0.1. The Google sample contains the first 20 distinct written reviews surfaced under the Korean phone filter in relevance order, spanning February 2019 through November 2026. Thirteen date to 2020. Developer replies were excluded. The examples describe uses expressed by Korean storefront reviewers. The sampling methods and review dates limit generalization to the 21 target countries.
